# Public Demo Audits

These reports are **independent public demonstration audits** of open-source repositories. They were not requested, commissioned, sponsored, approved, or endorsed by the upstream maintainers.

Their purpose is to demonstrate evidence-first exact-revision auditing without inventing customers or overstating what repository-visible evidence proves.

## What these demos prove

They show how the audit method preserves:
- exact-revision evidence;
- conditional/skipped CI semantics;
- bounded confidence instead of global green scoring;
- positive controls and limitations at the same time;
- explicit `UNVERIFIED` states when evidence is missing.

They do **not** establish that the upstream projects are insecure, defective, release-unready, or customers of AI Repository Audit.
